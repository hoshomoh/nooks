**Settings → General**, for admins. Four things live here.

## Name

What the instance calls itself — *Brunnen Street*, not *nooks*. It appears in the
sidebar, on printed sheets and on a public page, so a household name reads better than
a product name.

## Signup

Off by default. With it off there is no way in from outside: accounts are created by an
admin, and a temporary password is handed over in person.

Turn it on and somebody can ask for an account, which waits for an admin to approve.
It is worth leaving open only while the people who need accounts are creating them.

## Default language

The language of anything nobody chose a language for: the public page, a printed sheet,
and a member who has not picked one. Members choose their own, and theirs wins.

## The public page

One list, published read-only at a stable link. Alongside it:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show quantities and dates | On | The metadata a shopper actually needs |
| Show names | **Off** | Contributor names on the rows |
| Offer to join | Off | A quiet link at the foot for a visitor to ask for an account |

Names are off by default because a public page is about what needs buying rather than
about who lives at the address.

Unpublish and the link stops working. A visitor never sees any other list and cannot
discover that another one exists.

## Deleting the instance

At the foot of settings, and it does what it says: every list, item, member and token.
Back it up first — see [Back up](/docs/operations/back-up).
