---
title: Groups
description: A shortcut for sharing, and deliberately nothing more.
---

A group is a name and the people in it. *Flat*, *Parents*, *Weekend crew*.

Share a list with a group and everybody in it reaches the list. Add somebody to the
group later and they reach it too, without anybody going back through the lists to
find what they are missing.

## Making one

**Settings → Groups → New group**. Name it, and pick who is in it while you are there —
a group with nobody in it does nothing, so there is no reason to make one and then go
looking for the edit button.

## What a group is not

It has no lists of its own, no settings, and no permissions beyond who is in it. It
never becomes a hierarchy to maintain, because a household todo that needs an org chart
has stopped being a household todo.

That is why the groups page shows you the lists a group reaches. What a group reaches is
the whole of what a group does.

## Who can make them

Admins. A member can share a list with a group that exists, but making and changing
groups is instance administration.
