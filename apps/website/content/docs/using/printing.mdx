Most apps print a picture of themselves. Nooks prints a sheet you can take to a shop.

**Print** on any list, or `⌘P`. What comes out is an A4 page laid out for paper:

- The list's name at 27pt, with the date and an `N items · N people` line beside it.
- Items at 16pt with a **drawn checkbox** — a real box at 0.7pt, not a font glyph that
  prints as a filled square.
- Quantities in mono beside the label, because a quantity is what a shopper is actually
  reading.
- Three blank rows at the foot, for whatever gets remembered in the shop.

The sidebar, the chrome and the controls do not come with it. Nothing on the sheet is
a thing you cannot use on paper.

## What is on it

Completed items print too, ticked. A sheet that silently dropped what was already done
would be a sheet you cannot check against the trolley.

## Long lists

A list longer than a page continues onto the next one, with the title repeating. Items
are not split across the fold.
